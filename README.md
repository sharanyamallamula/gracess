# graces 

A wellness bullet journal made to look and feel like a real journal, complete with your very own plant companion "Grace" that grows alongside your habits!

Built for CS Girlies Hackathon by Sharanya (Ranya) Mallamula

## What graces does

graces is a daily journaling app themed around wellness and happiness. The goal is to make wellness tools feel more encouraging, instead of like one wrong move will make everything fall apart. You log your mood, habits, exercise, and add a journal entry each day.

## Features
- **Main Page** - mood tracker (1-5 scale), customizable habit tracker, exercise tracker and log, free-write journal entry, and an active to-do list
- **Grace** - a sprout companion that visually grows as you earn XP. XP is earned through logging habits, exercising, and submitting journal entries. Even if you miss a day, Grace won't die - she just won't grow that day, and that's okay!
- **Streaks** - while your streak does grow everyday, you will never be punished for missing a streak.
- **Pattern recognition** - the app compares your mood data against your habits, exercise, and journaling to create correlations between them! For example, it may find that your mood tends to be higher on days you exercise or complete habits.
- **Grace's Weekly Recap** - a short recap created by Grace that compiles the week's mood, habits, exercise, and streak as well as insights about the week.
- **Monthly grid view** - a full-month habit tracking grid and a color-coded mood calendar that lets you see your progress at a glance
- **Customizable habits & trackers** - ability to add whatever habits you want and your own trackers, as long as they work on a scale of 1-5
- **Long-term goals page** - a checklist of long-term goals that can also be numeric progress goals that you can slowly check off over time.

- ## Tech stach
- Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript were used!
- Data is stored through localStorage, so there is no backend or account needed.
